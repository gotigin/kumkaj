## The apps

Each Kumkaj product keeps shop or office books — sales, parties, day books,
menus or rates, staff — **on the devices where you install that app**. Local
use does not require a Kumkaj account. Optional shares (for example WhatsApp
of a receipt) leave the device only when you choose them.

## This website

kumkaj.com is a Makepad site with a thin static shell for legal pages and
link previews. We may use cookieless or first-party analytics when hosted —
no advertising trackers, no third-party ad cookie banner.

Contact links may open WhatsApp or your mail app. We do not run a lead form
on this host yet.
