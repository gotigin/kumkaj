# Kumkaj cancellation and refund policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

## Relationship to Gotigin

This policy is **additional** to the company policy at
[gotigin.com/cancellation](https://gotigin.com/cancellation).

**Kumkaj** is a brand of **GOTIGIN SOFTWARE & HARDWARE PRIVATE LIMITED** for
shop / hospitality products (**Moklanjo · Khaynjo · Kirano · Tabiyat**). The
Gotigin cancellation and refund policy is the baseline. This page adds
Kumkaj-brand detail for cloud and related paid offerings. Read both. Where
this page conflicts with the company policy on Kumkaj paid cloud use, **this
page controls** for that purchase — except that **mandatory law** always
controls.

Company: [gotigin.com/cancellation](https://gotigin.com/cancellation)  
Support: [support@gotigin.com](mailto:support@gotigin.com)

## Local apps vs cloud

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Local / free forever use** of a Kumkaj app on your devices | You may stop using or uninstall at any time. There is no Gotigin cloud fee to refund for pure local free use. |
| **Paid cloud plans** (Steady, Swift, Priority, Custom, and similar) | Sold as a **prepaid term** (typically a paid year). That term stands; see below. |

Plan catalogues, quotas, and end-of-term grace / retrieval behaviour are
described in product cloud documentation and the product site; they explain
**access life**, not a right to unwind payment mid-term.

## Current cloud term

Unless mandatory law requires otherwise:

- No self-serve mid-term cancel that refunds or prorates the **current** paid
  cloud period.
- Cloud access for that purchase continues through the paid term (and any
  documented grace), then follows the product’s end-of-term rules.
- **Renewal for a later period is never forced.** You may stop the next
  renewal so you are not billed for a future year.

## Custom quotes

Custom cloud pricing is agreed in writing (or checkout terms shown for that
quote). Cancellation and refund for Custom follow this brand policy and any
written quote terms; quote terms may add operational detail but do not remove
mandatory legal rights.
