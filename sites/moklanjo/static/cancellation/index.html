Last updated: 22 November 2026

Moklanjo is a Kumkaj product of GOTIGIN SOFTWARE & HARDWARE PRIVATE LIMITED. Those policies are the baseline. Where this page conflicts with the brand or company policy on Moklanjo paid cloud use, this page controls for that Moklanjo purchase — except that mandatory law always controls.

Support: support@gotigin.com

What you can stop anytime

  • Using the local Moklanjo app features that do not depend on a paid cloud plan.
  • Renewing into a later cloud period (next year is your choice).

What the current paid cloud plan means

Steady, Swift, Priority, Custom, and other paid Moklanjo cloud plans are prepaid for the stated term. Unless mandatory law requires otherwise, that purchase is not unwound mid-term by a self-serve cancel or automatic refund. You keep the cloud entitlement you paid for through that term (subject to the Moklanjo / Kumkaj terms and documented grace or end-of-term steps on the account / cloud docs).

Checkout may use Stripe, Paddle, or another partner shown at payment; their receipt and any binding consumer rules for that checkout apply to that buy.

App stores

If you paid Apple, Google, Microsoft, or another store for a Moklanjo-related item, that store’s refund process may apply to that channel in addition to this policy.

Exceptions

Email support@gotigin.com with subject hint Moklanjo cancellation or Moklanjo refund, plus account email and receipt. You will be heard. We decide case by case; a hearing is not a guaranteed refund.
