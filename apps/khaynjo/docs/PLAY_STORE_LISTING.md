## Full description (≤4000 characters)

```
Khaynjo is a restaurant operating system for dine-in restaurants, cafés, QSRs, counter shops, bakeries and cloud kitchens — built so lunch and dinner keep moving when the internet does not.

Your books live on the devices you install. There is no Kumkaj account required for day-to-day service. Use it free forever locally. Optional cloud, if you add it later, will not be required to take an order or close a bill.

WHO IT IS FOR
Owners who want the till, floor and kitchen on their own phones, tablets and PCs. Captains who punch at the table. Kitchen leads who need tickets without prices. A counter shop that never opens the floor still gets a clean order desk.

ORDER DESK
• Dine-in, takeaway, delivery and counter on one desk
• Fast path: pick dishes, send to kitchen, settle when ready
• Running order per table — hold, add, send only what’s new
• Variations and notes (size, toppings, less spicy) before Send KOT
• Settle with cash, UPI, card or mix; change, tip, part payment and Due (khata)
• Split tenders, move or merge tables, split a bill when you need to
• Print bill and show UPI QR for the amount when you configure it

FLOOR & TABLES
• Colour table tiles by area (AC, garden, parcel…) — time and running amount at a glance
• Visit flow: Free → Seated → In kitchen → Food ready → Served → Bill printed → Free when paid
• Seat guests before the first dish is punched
• Waiters on paired tablets can order and send KOT; taking payment stays rights-gated

KITCHEN & KOT
• Send KOT prints to the station you set — no one carries the slip
• Tickets without prices: table, waiter, notes, station routing
• Kitchen screen: New → Preparing → Ready → Completed, plus recall
• Cancel with reason; reprint a missed slip without inventing a new Send

MENU, BOOKS & DAY END
• Your menu, categories, photos, GST / cash-memo lanes, veg–non-veg marking
• Day book and reports the owner can trust — tenders, credit, item sales
• Purchases, expenses, stock and recipes when you grow into them
• Parties: customers (khata) and suppliers — credit is a real book, not a sticky note

TEAM & DEVICES
• Owner and staff unlock with PIN (optional login id + password)
• Rights for what each person may see and do — Floor, Kitchen, money screens
• One shop Server on your Wi‑Fi; extra tills and waiter tablets pair as Clients on the LAN — no cloud required for multi-device
• Bill & KOT printers on the shop devices you choose
• UI in many Indian languages (and more), including right-to-left where needed

BACKUP
Save a backup copy from Settings before you change phones or uninstall. On Android, uninstalling removes on-device books. A backup is a full shop file — keep it somewhere only the owner trusts.

START
Install → choose Server on the main till (or Client to join an existing shop) → set the Owner PIN → add your menu and start service.

Website: https://khaynjo.com
Privacy: see the Privacy policy URL on this listing

Khaynjo is part of the Kumkaj family of concrete business apps — local-first, free on your devices.
```
