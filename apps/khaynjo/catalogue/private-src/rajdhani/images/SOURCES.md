# Rajdhani pack photos — sources

Photos that ship with this pack (`images/…` in `template.json`). Each was
chosen by looking at the picture and is under a free licence: public domain /
CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA. CC BY and CC BY-SA ask for the author and licence to
be named — this file is that record; the publish script puts a copy beside the
published pack (`…/public/templates/rajdhani/SOURCES.md`).

Every photo was resized to at most 1024 px and saved as WebP; two were cropped
(noted). Add a row here whenever a photo is added, and never add a photo
without a licence that allows reuse.
