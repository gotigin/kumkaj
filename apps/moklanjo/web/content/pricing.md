## Free forever locally

No charge per rider, user, parcel, seat or local device. Run Moklanjo on as many office devices as you need.

## Kumkaj Cloud — planned

Optional backup and sync will meter storage, transfer and requests — **same rates for everyone**. It will never be required to book a parcel. Read [Kumkaj principles](https://kumkaj.com/principles/).
