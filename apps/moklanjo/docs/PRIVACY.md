## Local-first — what stays on your device

Moklanjo is built for **offline / local use**. The office's books —
bookings, AWBs, rates, parties, runs and bags, COD, day book, staff,
settings — are stored **on the devices where you install Moklanjo**.

- You do **not** need a Kumkaj account to use the app locally.
- We do **not** require cloud sync for booking or day-to-day desk use.
- Optional Kumkaj Cloud (if offered later) will be separate and opt-in.

## What the app stores

Typical data on the device includes:

- Business setup (office name, logo, tax / country settings, printers)
- Consignors, consignees and other parties you enter: names, phone numbers,
  addresses, pincodes, optional logos and custom fields
- Bookings: AWB numbers, destinations, weights and sizes, contents,
  declared value and other docket fields, charges, payments, COD amounts
- Tracking history of each parcel, delivery notes, proof-of-delivery photos
  and signatures, and — only when the device provides it — the location
  where a delivery was recorded
- Riders and carriers, runs and bags, rider COD hand-ins
- Party ledgers, expenses, carrier bills, notes and attachments you add
- Staff names, roles, and **PIN / password hashes** (not plaintext PINs)
- Local backup copies under the app's data folder

Uninstalling the Android app usually **deletes** this on-device data. Save a
backup before you uninstall or change phones.

## Backups you export

From Settings → Backup you can save a backup file (SQLite). That file is a
**full copy of the office books**, including the names, phone numbers and
addresses of the people you send parcels for, and it is **not encrypted**.
Store it only where the owner trusts (phone storage, USB, your Drive).
Anyone with the file can read it; short PIN hashes can be attacked offline —
change PINs if a backup leaks.

## What leaves the device

Data leaves the device only when **you** choose to, for example:

- **Save a backup copy…** / restore from a file you pick
- Share or print a receipt, label, bill or run sheet (system share sheet,
  WhatsApp or a printer you configure); a WhatsApp or SMS message is sent
  only from your own phone, after you open or copy it
- Export a file you ask for (CSV, Tally vouchers, reports)
- Pair **extra counters and rider phones on your office LAN** (Server ↔
  Client on your Wi‑Fi) — traffic stays on your network, not a Kumkaj
  cloud
- Optional features you turn on later (e.g. cloud) — described when
  available

**On the office Wi‑Fi**, the Server also answers two pages for people on
that network:

- **Track** (`/track`): anyone on the office Wi‑Fi who types an AWB number
  sees that parcel's status, destination, consignee name, weight, booking
  time and tracking history.
- **Book** (`/book`, for account customers): a company books a parcel to
  its own account.

The app serves them only on the office network; it does not put them on the
internet.

We do not sell office data or use it for advertising.
