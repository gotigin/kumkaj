# Tabiyat cancellation and refund policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

## Relationship to brand and company

This policy is **additional** to:

1. [gotigin.com/cancellation](https://gotigin.com/cancellation) (company), and  
2. [kumkaj.com/cancellation](https://kumkaj.com/cancellation) (Kumkaj brand).

LIMITED**. Those policies are the baseline. This page adds Tabiyat-specific
detail for [tabiyat.shop](https://tabiyat.shop) and the Tabiyat app
(including cloud shop and account billing). Read them together. Where this
page conflicts with the brand or company policy on Tabiyat paid cloud use,
**this page controls** for that Tabiyat purchase — except that **mandatory
law** always controls.

## What you can stop anytime

- Using the **local** Tabiyat app features that do not depend on a paid cloud
  plan.
- **Renewing** into a later cloud period (next year is your choice).

## What the current paid cloud plan means

Steady, Swift, Priority, Custom, and other paid Tabiyat cloud plans are
**prepaid for the stated term**. Unless mandatory law requires otherwise,
that purchase is not unwound mid-term by a self-serve cancel or automatic
refund. You keep the cloud entitlement you paid for through that term
(subject to the Tabiyat / Kumkaj terms and documented grace or end-of-term
steps on the account / cloud docs).

Checkout may use Paddle or another partner shown at payment; their
receipt and any binding consumer rules for that checkout apply to that buy.

## App stores

If you paid Apple, Google, Microsoft, or another store for a Tabiyat-related
item, that store’s refund process may apply to **that** channel in addition
to this policy.

## Exceptions

Email [support@gotigin.com](mailto:support@gotigin.com) with subject hint
`Tabiyat cancellation` or `Tabiyat refund`, plus account email and receipt.
You will be heard. We decide case by case; a hearing is not a guaranteed
refund.
