**Gotigin** builds the Kumkaj family of business operating systems.

We ship **concrete** products for a trade — not a single generic till that every owner must bend into shape. Today that means:

- **Moklanjo** (*to send*, in Memoni) — courier and logistics agency desk
- **Khaynjo** (*food*, in Memoni) — restaurant, café and QSR service

The only shared platform is the Makepad 2 workspace each app builds on. There is no Kumkaj “core” product crate: each app owns its books and flows so a fix for restaurants never ripples into couriers.
