## Software adapts to users

Each product is concrete for its trade. Moklanjo is a courier desk. Khaynjo is a restaurant system. We do not ship one vague “works for anyone” app that the owner must reinterpret.

## Local-first

The counter keeps working when the internet stops. Books, tickets and day close live on your devices.

## Your data, your device

Sales, parties and reports stay in the shop. Extra tills join over your own Wi‑Fi with a pairing code.

## Free forever locally

No price per seat, menu item, table or local device. Run it free on Android, Windows and Linux.

## Cloud never holds data hostage

[Kumkaj Cloud](/cloud/) is planned. When it exists, you pay only for storage, transfer and requests you use — same rates for everyone. Cloud is optional; the local app remains complete without it.
