**Status: planned.** There is no Cloud sign‑up yet.

When Kumkaj Cloud ships, it will meter only what you use — storage, transfer and requests — at the same rates for every shop. It will not charge per seat, table, rider or local device.

What Cloud will **not** do:

- Hold your books hostage — the local app stays complete offline
- Replace your shop Wi‑Fi for day‑to‑day tills and kitchen screens
- Invent a second “cloud‑only” product that your staff must relearn

Until Cloud exists, keep backups on devices you control. See [Security](/security/).
