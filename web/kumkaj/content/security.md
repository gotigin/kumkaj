## On your devices

Moklanjo and Khaynjo keep sales, parties, menus and day books in a local database on each device. There is no required Kumkaj account to run the shop.

## Extra tills on the shop Wi‑Fi

Additional counters, captain tablets and kitchen screens join over **your** Wi‑Fi with a pairing code. Today that LAN link does **not** use TLS — said plainly. Treat the shop network as trusted floor equipment; do not expose till ports to the public internet.

Multi‑device LAN behaviour is proven in software and still completing a real‑hardware pilot. Product sites only claim what that pilot has cleared.

## Backups

Export and restore from the apps so a lost phone or PC does not take the books with it. Cloud backup is [planned](/cloud/), not required.
