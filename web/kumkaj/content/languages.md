Moklanjo and Khaynjo ship with **29 languages** on every screen. Arabic and Urdu run right‑to‑left. Receipts and kitchen tickets can print in the script your kitchen reads.

This website’s own copy is English first. Hindi and Gujarati pages come next after native review — many owners already use browser translate in the meantime.
