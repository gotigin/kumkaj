Full description
----------------
Kumkaj is Gotigin’s local-first business operating system for tea stalls, clothes counters, restaurants, and other shops.

• Sell with Cash, UPI, Card, or Credit
• Actual / Applied / Rounded totals for honest bill rounding
• Owner & Staff PINs with immutable sale authorship
• Optional tables and kitchen tickets, sales amend history, backup & restore
• Works offline — your data stays on the device
