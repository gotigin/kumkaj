Full description
----------------
Kumkaj Courier (kumkaj.com) is a local-first courier business operating system:
party ledgers, day book, and a booking desk for walk-ins, AWB/barcode, and tracking.

• Books: parties, charges, day book, payouts
• Booking desk for counter walk-ins
• Works offline — your data stays on the device
