# Kumkaj

Kumkaj (kumkaj.com) — local-first business operating system on Makepad 2.

One app for a tea stall, a clothes counter, a restaurant, or any other shop that needs to sell, keep a catalogue, take payment, and see the day. Restaurant tables and kitchen tickets stay available when a food business turns them on. They are not the product.

**App ids:** production `com.kumkaj.courier` · development `com.kumkaj.courier.debug` ([`apps/courier/env/`](apps/courier/env/), baked by `apps/courier/build.rs`). Platform matrix and data paths: [`docs/DEV.md`](docs/DEV.md).
